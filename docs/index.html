Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Drawing Board handles information when you use the app.

We respect your privacy and have designed the app so that drawings and other content created while using the app can remain stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address in order to use the app.

We do not collect or store your drawings, sketches, notes, or other creative content on our servers.

3. Local Storage

Drawings and other content created in the app may be stored locally on your device.

This information is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Because your drawings are stored locally, we do not have access to them and cannot recover them if they are deleted or lost from your device.

4. Advertising and Analytics

The app does not display advertisements and does not use third-party advertising services.

The app does not use third-party analytics services to track your activity or usage for analytics purposes.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or creative content with third parties.

Because your drawings and other content are not uploaded to our servers, we do not have access to them.

6. Photos and Media

If the app provides features that allow you to import images or save drawings to your device, access to photos or media may be requested when necessary to provide those features.

Images and media accessed through these features are used only for the requested functionality and are not uploaded to our servers by the app.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete drawings and other locally stored content using the available features within the app, where applicable.

You may also remove the app and its locally stored data from your device. Deleting the app may permanently remove locally stored drawings and other content.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be updated accordingly.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of the app, please contact us at:
